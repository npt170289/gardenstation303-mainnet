Information We May Process

Depending on the features you use, we may process:

  • Pi account information, such as Pi username, UID, or authentication-related information, when you sign in through Pi.
  • Booking information voluntarily submitted through the reservation form, such as your name, contact details, requested date and time, party size, and booking notes.
  • Messages or questions submitted through the chatbot.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the application.

Please do not include sensitive personal information in booking notes or chatbot messages unless it is necessary and you have a lawful basis to provide it.

Purposes of Processing

We process information to:

  • Display restaurant menus, services, and business information.
  • Receive, manage, and respond to booking requests.
  • Answer menu and restaurant-related questions.
  • Authenticate users and provide Pi-related application functions.
  • Process and verify Pi transactions where those features are enabled.
  • Maintain security, prevent misuse, and comply with legal obligations.

Booking and Supabase

The booking feature uses Supabase infrastructure to store and process reservation information. Information submitted through the booking form may be transmitted to and stored by that service provider for reservation management.

Supabase’s own terms and privacy documentation also apply to its processing activities.

Chatbot

The chatbot provides automated responses about restaurant information and menu items. Chatbot responses are informational and may occasionally be incomplete or inaccurate. Do not submit payment credentials, wallet private keys, passwords, or unnecessary sensitive information through the chatbot.

Pi Network and Payments

Pi authentication and Pi payment functionality may involve Pi Network services. When a Pi transaction is initiated, relevant transaction information may be processed by the Pi platform and recorded on the applicable blockchain. Blockchain records may be public and may not be editable or removable by the application operator.

We do not request or need your Pi Wallet passphrase or private keys.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers, Pi Network services, or other service providers where necessary to deliver the application. We may also disclose information when required by law or necessary to protect lawful rights and security.

Retention and Security

Booking and other personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy or as required by law. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, deletion of, or restriction of processing of your personal data, and may withdraw consent where applicable.
